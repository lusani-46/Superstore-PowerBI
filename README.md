# Superstore Power BI Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship Programme – Week 2: Business Analytics Case Study**.

The objective of the project was to transform Superstore retail transaction data into an interactive **Microsoft Power BI dashboard** that helps management monitor sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product performance, and regional performance.

## Business Questions

The dashboard addresses the following business questions:

1. What is the overall sales performance of the company?
2. Which regions generate the highest sales and profit?
3. Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
4. Which product categories perform best?
5. Which products are the most profitable?
6. What trends can be observed over time?
7. What recommendations should management implement to improve business performance?

## Key Business Insights

* The **West region** is the strongest-performing region in both sales and profit.
* The **Consumer segment** contributes the largest share of company sales.
* **Technology** is the strongest product category in terms of sales and profit.
* **Furniture** generates substantial sales but has a relatively low profit margin.
* The business generated approximately **$2.30M in sales and $286.40K in profit**, resulting in a **12.5% overall profit margin**.

## Business Risks

1. Low profitability in the Furniture category.
2. Loss-making products affecting overall profitability.
3. Performance gap between stronger and weaker regions, particularly the South region.

## Business Opportunities

1. Expand successful strategies from the West region into weaker-performing regions.
2. Continue investing in high-performing and profitable product categories.
3. Increase customer value through retention, cross-selling, and targeted engagement.

## Management Recommendations

1. Review Furniture pricing and discount levels.
2. Investigate loss-making products and their cost structures.
3. Strengthen sales and marketing initiatives in the South region.
4. Continue investing in high-performing categories such as Technology.
5. Expand high-performing customer segments through targeted retention and cross-selling initiatives.
